How it works

From “where do I start?” to offer‑ready

No pre-written course. Claude writes every lesson for you and adapts as you go.

1

Tell Ace where you are

Your role, experience, target job and how comfortable you are with each subject. Ace designs a 10-level roadmap whose Level 1 starts at your level — no re-learning what you already know.

2

Learn, try, practise

Each lesson is a short read you can question, a hands-on warm-up, then adaptive questions. Every answer updates your mastery, so the next lesson targets your gaps.

3

Interview with confidence

Prep for a specific company from its job description, then run mock interviews that score you like a hiring panel — and keep climbing past level 10.

New

Lessons you can question

Every lesson opens with a short, textbook-style read written for your gaps. Stuck on a sentence? Highlight it.

  • Explain more, give an example, simplify or show the interview angle — in one click
  • Or type your own question; Ace answers in a side panel that knows the whole lesson
  • Re-read the lesson any time, even in the middle of the quiz
New

Learn by doing before you're tested

Between the reading and the questions, a hands-on warm-up makes the ideas stick.

  • Flashcards you answer in your head, then flip
  • Worked examples revealed one step at a time
  • Quick checks — “predict what happens” moments that don't count against you
  • Sort it out challenges, like safe vs risky HashMap keys
New

Practise like Duolingo, think like an interviewer

Seven question types, graded instantly — short answers are graded by Claude the way a real interviewer listens.

  • Hints unlock after a few seconds, so you try first (hinted answers earn half XP)
  • Go back to any earlier answer to review it — your current question stays as you left it
  • Wrong answers come back at the end for a second chance
  • Multiple choice, select-all, true/false, fill-in-the-blanks, short answer, ordering and match pairs
New

A roadmap with no ceiling

Ten levels from where you are today to expert, architect-level depth. Finish them and Ace keeps going.

  • Everyone starts at Level 1 — pitched at “New to it”, “Comfortable” or “Advanced”
  • Test out of any level with its checkpoint
  • After level 10, Ace designs levels 11, 12, 13… around what you've mastered and where you're still weak
  • The coach can add a remedial topic to your path when it spots a missing prerequisite

FAQ

Good questions

What do I need to use Ascend?

A Claude Code sign-in (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise) — run claude in a terminal and use /login — or an Anthropic API key, which you can add in Settings. Ascend bundles Claude Code, so there's nothing else to install.

Does it cost anything to use?

Ascend itself is a download from GitHub. Lessons, grading and interviews run on your own Claude account, so they count towards your plan's usage — or your API bill if you use a key. Settings shows how much it has used, and a lighter model or “Faster” mode stretches it further.

I already know the basics — do I have to start from scratch?

No. Tell Ace you're “Comfortable” or “Advanced” and your Level 1 starts there, skipping what you already know. You can also test out of any level by passing its checkpoint.

What happens after level 10?

Your roadmap keeps growing. The first 10 levels are the core path; once you finish them, Ace designs the next levels — deeper internals, specialisations and current practice — around what you've mastered and where you're still weak.

Where is my data stored?

On your computer, in a local file with a daily backup. Settings has export, import and reset. Your prompts go to Claude to generate lessons, exactly as when you use Claude Code yourself.

Why does Windows or macOS warn me when I open it?

The builds aren't code-signed or notarized yet, so your system asks you to confirm the first launch. The download section above shows the one-time steps. Every build is produced by GitHub Actions straight from the public source code.
